 Issue 4:

Open this User Menu. (Note that the menu shows the shortcut key.)  
 Pin at least 1 item.  
 Ensure that the “More…” submenu has at least 2 items.  
Open the menu again.  
 \*\* Now the menu does not show the shortcut key.
