No need to launch Karaoki or another standalone karaoke player — [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) has you covered, soup to nuts.

DEX 3’s skin is multifaceted and robust with on-screen tabs for 2-deck, 4-deck, simple and variations of each (with/without video, sampler decks, etc). What doesn’t currently install with the application, at least for now, is a basic karaoke-specific skin.
