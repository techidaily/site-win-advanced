### Here’s a look at some of the key features included with the DDJ-SR2:

**Performance Pads**  
Large rubber performance pads located below the big jog wheel provide hot cue and sampler functions. Each soft-touch rubber pad illuminates in blue to enable users to tap the pads quickly for each effect, with the output volume varying depending on the strength of each tap.

**Big Jog Wheels**  
The DDJ-SR2 features two large jog wheels for precise scratching and mixing capabilities. The resistance for each jog wheel was fined tuned to provide users with the best feel and the shortest latency.

**2-Channel Mixer**  
The built-in 2-channel mixer on the DDJ-SR2 is ideal for scratch performances. The mixer can also be used with four decks using the deck select buttons located on each side of the controller. In addition, the unit features a cross fader reverse switch to enable the left and right decks to be controlled from opposite sides.

**Other notable DDJ-SR2 features:**

* 2-channel DJ controller designed for use with Serato DJ
* Compact size
* Large brushed-aluminum, touch-capacitive jog wheels
* Onboard effects and rubber performance pads
* Visual beat indicator

**Specifications**

* Channels: 2
* Bit resolution: 24-bit
* Sampling rate: 96kHz
* Frequency response: 20Hz – 20kHz  
**Connectivity**
* Phono inputs: 2 (RCA)
* CD/Line inputs: 2 (RCA)
* Mic inputs 1
* USB ports: 2
* Booth out: 1 (RCA)
* Master out: 2 (RCA, XLR)  
**Onboard Features**
* Displays: 3 touchscreen
* Jog wheels: Yes
* Touch strip Yes
* Mixer: 4-channel/4-deck
* EQ: 3-band
* Filter control: Yes
* Built-in FX: Yes
* Trigger pads 16 velocity sensitive  
**Dimensions**
* Width: 21.8 in.
* Height: 2.57 in.
* Depth: 12.56 in.
* Weight: 9.92 lb.
